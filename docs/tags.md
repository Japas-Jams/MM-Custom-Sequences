# Rules on how to set tags to songs

- Location tags are **only** for BGM.
- Fanfare type and fanfare length tags are **only** for Fanfares.
- Ignore the "fanfare length" tag, since that one will be defined later.
- Respect the "required" and "optional" in parentheses when assigning tags. For example, a BGM can have a maximum of 2 location, 2 moods, and 2 themes; and also can have a minimum of 1 location and 1 mood.


# Tags

We define 3 tiers of tags, with different purposes:

## LOCATION *(required 1 or 2)*
Defines in which **context** the song should appear. Is the current implementation in every randomizer.

### Field ⛰️
The song appears in open areas. Should motivate exploring, travel and adventure. Should **NOT** be repetitive.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Hyrule Field
>
> Okami - Shinshuu Plains
>
> Kirby and the Forgotten Land - Running Through a New World

### Town 🏘️
This song appears in civilization, like towns, villages and cities; places with plenty of people. Styles may vary wildly, but is never intense.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Kakariko Village
>
> Final Fantasy VII - Cosmo Canyon
>
> Super Mario RPG - Hello, Happy Kingdom
>
> Clair Obscur Expedition 33 - Lumiere

### Interiors 🛖
This songs appears in interior locations, like houses, shops or refuges. Should feel safe and/or welcoming, even quirky.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - House
>
> Nintendo Wii - Wii Shop Channel
>
> The Sims - Mall Rat
>
> Resident Evil 2 - Secure Place 

### Minigame 🎯
This song appears in minigames, parties and bonuses. Should be active and fun.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Mini-Game
>
> Donkey Kong 64 - Bonus Barrel
>
> Mario Party - Move to the Mambo!

### Dungeon 🏛️
This song appears on dungeons, castles, temples, ruins, mazes, caverns and the like. Should evoke an sense of mystery and otherwordlyness, help you think to solve puzzles. Never too intense, and **NEVER** repetitive.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Forest Temple
>
> Yoshi's Island - Castle & Fortress
>
> Professor Layton - Puzzle


### Fight ⚔️
This song appears on confrontations, battles, bosses or fights. Styles may vary wildly, but always should be action oriented.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Ganondorf Battle
>
> Pokémon Omega Ruby & Alpha Sapphire - Vs. Zinnia
>
> Ace Attorney Investigations - Lying Coldly
>
> Paper Mario - Tubba Blubba

### Character 🧙‍♂️
This song appears as a character theme, or in a important ocassion or events. It can never play for too long, so they are usually short. Styles vary wildly.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Shiek's Theme
>
> Ace Attorney - Turnabout Sisters
>
> Final Fantasy VI - Kefka
>
> Undertale - sans.


## MOOD *(required 1 or 2)*
Defines the ***vibes*** the song should give. It should match with the place the song is located.

### Happy
The song has a happy or heroic feel.

### Gloomy
The song has a sad or evil feel.

### Calm
The song has a calm or mystic feel.


## THEME *(optional 1 or 2)*
Defines the ***thematic or ambiance*** of the song. Related to location, but in a more specific way. Not applicable in all instances, hence is optional.

### Dry 🏜️
The song appears on deserts, pyramids, or sand related places. Usually have an oriental feel.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Gerudo Valley
>
> Super Mario Bros 2 - Cave
>
> Banjo-Kazooie - Gobi's Valley
>
> Mario Party 3 - Spiny Desert

### Hot 🌋
The song appears on hot or lava related places.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Fire Temple
>
> Star Fox 64 - Solar
>
> New Super Mario Bros - Castle
>
> Pokémon Snap - Volcano

### Snowy ❄️
The song appears on cold, winter or ice related places. Usually have a magical or Chrismas feel.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Ice Cavern
>
> Banjo-Kazooie - Freezeezy Peak
>
> Diddy Kong Racing - Frosty Village
>
> Donkey Kong Country 2 - In a Snowbound Land

### Nature 🌿
The song appears on forests, jungles or plant related places. Usually have a tropical, reggae or magical feel.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Kokiri Forest
>
> New Super Mario Bros - Jungle
>
> Super Mario RPG - Beware of the Forest Mushrooms
>
> Yoshi's Island - Overworld

### Watery 🌊
The song appears underwater, or moist places, like beaches, rivers or swamps.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Zora's Domain
>
> Super Mario World - Underwater
>
> Kirby's Return to Dream Land - Rainy Area
>
> Super Mario Kart - Koopa Beach

### Spooky 🎃
This song appears on scary places, like cementeries, mansions or churches. Usually have an horror or Halloween feel.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Shadow Temple
>
> Banjo-Kazooie - Mad Monster Mansion
>
> Deltarune - A DARK ZONE
>
> Bloodborne - Cleric Beast

### Goofy 🎪
This song appears on fun places. Usually related to carnivals and clowns.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Windmill Hut
>
> Super Mario 3D World - Switch Scramble Circus
>
> Freddy Fazbear's Pizzeria Simulator - Just Add Water
>
> Mario Paint - Creative Exercise

### Howdy 🐎
This song appears on ranches, or cowboy related places. Usually have a western or fiddly feel, with heavy use of banjo and fiddle.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Lon Lon Ranch
>
> Final Fantasy VII - Fiddle De Chocobo
>
> Zelda Twilight Princess - Hidden Village
>
> Stardew Valley - Pelican Town

### Magical ✨
This song appears on magical, mystical or divine places. Usually have a ethereal and otherworldly feel.
> *Examples:*
>
> Zelda Ocarina of Time - Fairy Fountain
>
> Zelda Twilight Princess - City in the Sky
>
> Donkey Kong Country 2 - Forest Interlude
>
> Persona 5 - Aria Of The Soul

**The following are only available for FANFARES:**

## FANFARE TYPE *(requiered 1 or 2)*
Defines the **context** of a fanfare, in a similar way to locations do to BGM.

### Collect 🧩
This fanfare apears when picking up collectables or items. Are usually very short.

### Victory 🏆
This fanfare appears on wins.

### Defeat 💀
This fanfare appears on defeats, like game overs or losing minigames.

## FANFARE LENGTH *(required 1)*
Metadata that controls the length of the fanfare, so it's not too short or long depending on the context.

### Short
This fanfare has a short duration.

### Long
This fanfare has a long duration.
